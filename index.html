Installation of New Leaders

Ukarimu Ministries Trust

📅 23rd August 2025 | 🕜 1:30 pm – 4:30 pm

📍 Ukarimu House

Program Schedule

1. Pre-Ceremony Arrangements – 40 mins

Guest Arrivals

Guests welcomed by ushers and directed to seats. Bitings served.

2. Opening Segment – 10 mins

MC opens with prayer, greetings, and program outline. Invocation/Reflection (5 mins).

3. Acknowledgments & Introductions – 20 mins

Recognition of board members & partners. Overview of UMT history, founders, and reasons for new leadership.

4. Installation Ceremony – 20 mins

  • New Directors: Ian & Stella Orunga – Installed by Kingsburys, prayer, token of appreciation.
  • Marriage & Family Coordinators: Barnabas & Grace Achoki – Installed by Pst. Joel Chola. Partnership agreement signing, token of appreciation.
  • Partnership Liaison: Amanda Mugwe – Installed by Pst. Andrew Kariuki, token of appreciation.

5. Keynote Speech – 30 mins

Rev. Miano Weche, Daystar Chaplain.

6. Honouring Outgoing Directors – 20 mins

Appreciation & accomplishments (10 mins). Token of appreciation. Outgoing directors’ remarks (10 mins).

7. Closing Segment – 20 mins

  • Vote of thanks by MC (5 mins)
  • Benediction: Pst. Omeno Suji (5 mins)
  • Cake ceremony – Nita Kihiro (10 mins)
